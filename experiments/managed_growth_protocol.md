# Managed growth pilot, 25 November 2026

Can an external inspector anticipate which distinction a fixed budget would cost, and should it permit one more group? This is a two-output, scripted study of the working direction/strength learner's local update and review rules. The shared resource is the number of active local prediction groups. Fine-address storage is identical. The upper predictor is outside the budget and the score.

Use 24 paired environment seeds (4100–4123). Sample two independent uniform four-address inputs per tick, with two randomly assigned addresses per named source on each channel. During 256 acquisition ticks, A/B predict success at .9/.9 and C/D at .8/.2. Change B to .1 for 384 ticks. Either retain C/D's difference or change both to .5 throughout the rest of the run. Restore B to .9 for a final 192 ticks. Cross conditions on the same latent draws, underlying address mappings and proposal seeds. Reviews receive 96 independent labelled rows every 64 ticks. No truth, future labels or condition identifiers enter learner or inspector.

Use ordinary native proposals, acceptances, memory weakening and transfers. The one experiment-only constraint applies after the two local proposals, before downstream review: if both splits would exceed the three-group budget, compare the two feasible single merges on the current review batch and pool the channel with smaller increase in log loss. Clear its pending reduction. The four-group arm never needs this constraint. No externally requested split. Start pooled, learn during acquisition, and include every seed regardless of acquisition success.

Immediately before review at tick 384, inspect fast evidence by fine address. For each channel estimate the extra Brier loss from pooling instead of its best available binary partition using those weighted frequencies. Forecast the cheaper channel to pool, the specific cost of pooling the other task to accommodate an A/B split, and the mean benefit of a fourth group (half the smaller cost). Permit four if the projected mean benefit exceeds .01. This threshold is a practical design choice. Log the forecast before branching. Make this one choice for the rest of the run.

Fork the same state before review. Compare three versus four on identical remaining data, and read the inspector-selected arm from those paired paths. Log actual group membership after reviews, including permission without use. Score one-step Brier on A, B, C and D separately; average channel scores equally. Primary window 385–640, return window 641–832. Report fixed-minus-permitted paired gains and fixed-minus-selected gains; group ticks above three measure utilisation. Compare against always permitting four on loss and permission frequency. Inspect which channel is actually pooled and whether B recovers after return. Resample paired environment seeds for uncertainty. Pilot evidence of selectivity requires correct permission choices in at least 18/24 per condition, positive mean gain for selected versus fixed in the valuable condition, and mean selected return harm below .01 in both conditions. Report exceptions and actual forecast accuracy against the observed .01 gain separately.
