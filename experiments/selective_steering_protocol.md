## Prerequisite: distinguish refresh from steering

The experimental subclass refits active predictions at the END of each review.
It does not change within-review candidate selection, memory admission or
weakening decisions. An uncontrolled refreshed variant is compared with the
frozen original combined engine on every fresh trajectory: every pre-update
probability and ordinary review decision must match (floating tolerance1e-12
for probabilities,1e-9 for arithmetic replay). Final costs differ by one refit
per review; current fitted models may differ immediately after a review.

Fixtures also replay the five saved032 immediate-effect failures: a refresh-only
operation reproduces them, and after refresh a local weakening has no additional
immediate composite effect. No new labels are used for this check. Independent
fits verify coherent snapshots after every refreshed review. This is a narrow
snapshot repair, not a fix to any within-review stale-model comparison.

## Controls and semantics

After review512, issue a supplied instruction until review1024:
* Lock the protected local channel's current partition. Continue learning its
  probabilities and fast/slow evidence, migration and automatic weakening.
  Probability-ordered group IDs may relabel; membership equivalence is held.
  Skip structural proposals only in that channel. Clear its structural pending
  decision when locking or releasing. A wrong acquired partition stays wrong.
* Make the other local channel more plastic by reducing ONLY its slow-evidence
  half-life from1024 to128 ticks. Fast half-life64 and all other rules stay fixed.
* At review1024 release all locks and restore slow half-lives1024. No snapshot
  restoration, artificial evidence or retroactive undo of forgetting.

The composite channel retains the original rules. A local improvement need not
improve composite prediction. Locking estimates, locking interfaces, preserving
an autonomous core of named forms, and learned selection of controls are outside
scope. Commands include reasons and are checkpointed/inspectable; inspection is
a detached copy. Controls are supplied by the experimenter, not inferred from
hidden world truth by the learner.
