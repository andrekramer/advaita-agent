# Next round: relationships, interfaces, and learning how to learn

Started 2026-10-20. The ambition is:

**Learning distinctions → learning relationships → learning interfaces →
learning how to learn.**

Creation, retention, reopening, and collapse belong at every level. Learning
what to ignore is part of the enquiry, not merely a consequence of a smaller
model. Distinguish temporarily ignoring detail, hiding it behind an interface,
and deleting it: they have different recovery costs.

The first round established bounded form acquisition, selective collapse, and
reacquisition within a supplied language. It did not establish a need for two
timescales, robust reciprocal complementarity, learned interfaces, or
meta-learning. The collapse/reacquisition test exposed a temporary prediction
cost. See the [evidence synthesis](../results/research-synthesis.md).

## Stage 1: useful relationships

[Experiment 010](bundles_protocol.md) starts with a small stochastic world and
stochastic structural proposals. A minimal bundle is a selected pair of
observable distinctions with a joint action-conditioned outcome table. The
learner must discover which pair matters, stop using an unnecessary member,
and recover the dependency when it returns. A persistent-dependency control
tests preservation under the same environmental noise.

This is feature-subset structure learning, not discovery of perceptual
primitives, a category hierarchy, temporal state discovery, or a general bundle
architecture. The task deliberately permits a direct two-feature proposal:
requiring every intermediate single-feature proposal to help would obstruct
discovery of a pure interaction. The first run does not use nested Markov
matrices, oscillator phases, or an LLM.

Prediction reads only selected features. Proposal search can inspect the full
bounded observation buffer. That measures selective use, not reduced sensing,
secure encapsulation, or learning a fold. Randomness generates proposals;
observed outcomes and a fixed complexity cost select retention. Deterministic
search receives the same candidate budget. Internal randomness is a hypothesis
to compare, not an assumed advantage.

## Stage 2: folds and interfaces — conditional proposal

Choose one operation first: aggregation of internal states, or a return-trace
interface. Specify which action-conditioned predictions, durations, and costs
must be preserved. Compare against the full internal model and an inadequate
reduction. Test whether a compact interface can be retained, challenged, and
reopened, including rare consequential differences and temporary convergence.

The interface must have a measurable cost or access restriction; merely
renaming a subset does not demonstrate information hiding. Trace chains hide
intermediate visits and change the clock; aggregation combines states and need
not preserve the Markov property. Neither operation alone supplies a rule for
learning the underlying distinctions.

## Stage 3: meta-learning — conditional proposal

Permit one bounded revision mechanism to change through experience, such as
proposal preferences, review timing, or retention versus deletion. Train on
task families and evaluate subsequent adaptation on held-out task families.
Freeze the learned outer mechanism during evaluation while ordinary task
learning continues. Include a reset-meta-state control and a fixed strategy
selected under the same training budget.

Meta-learning means experience improves subsequent learning. It can occur
through learned initialisation or memory even with a fixed update rule;
hyperparameter changes are neither necessary nor sufficient evidence.

## Influences and scope

The [four influences](../roadmap.md#four-influences-for-consolidation) supply
questions: Grossberg about complementary contributions, Kelso about stability
and changing repertoires, Friston about interfaces and model revision, and QBism
about expectations tested through action and experience. They are not unified
or validated by the proposed experiments. Markov traces and probabilistic
causality remain mathematical resources to examine.

Each stage needs a fixed protocol, bounded resources, controls, and an audit.
Experiment 010 is the only benchmark authorised by this initial execution
plan. Stop after reporting its outcome; do not tune or automatically progress
to folds, meta-learning, additional task families, or larger models.
